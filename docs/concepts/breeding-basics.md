# Breeding basics

This page provides only the concepts needed to understand BreedOps.

## Parent

Material used to generate progeny through a cross.

## Cross

A recorded mating/crossing event between selected parents.

## Family

A progeny group derived from a cross.

## Seed lot

A physical harvested batch of seeds associated with a lineage.

## Phenotype

An observed characteristic of biological material under particular conditions.

## Selection

Choosing which candidates continue in the breeding process based on explicit
objectives and evidence.

BreedOps stores lineage and evidence separately so later decisions remain
traceable.
