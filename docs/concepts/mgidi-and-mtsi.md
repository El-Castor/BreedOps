# MGIDI and MTSI

## MGIDI

MGIDI is a multi-trait selection index. It reduces correlated trait information
into latent factors and ranks candidates according to distance from an
ideotype.

It is useful when several traits must improve together, but it still depends on
the quality and meaning of the input traits.

## MTSI

MTSI extends multi-trait selection to stability/performance information across
environments. In BreedOps it relies on compatible WAASBY analysis results.

## What neither method proves by itself

Neither method automatically estimates:

- additive breeding value;
- genetic correlation;
- expected additive genetic gain.

Those claims require additional genetic and experimental structure.
