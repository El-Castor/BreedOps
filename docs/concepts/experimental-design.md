# Experimental design

Statistical software cannot repair an experiment that lacks the replication or
structure needed to answer the question.

Before analysing:

- identify the experimental unit;
- define entries/genotypes;
- define environments;
- record replication/block information when available;
- keep measurement occasions explicit;
- avoid merging distinct biological units only because their names look alike.

BreedOps performs eligibility checks for several analyses and may refuse to
estimate unsupported quantities.
