# 5 — Families and seed lots

## Family

A family is the biological progeny group derived from a cross. In BreedOps it
links downstream material back to the crossing event.

## Seed lot

A seed lot represents a concrete harvested lot associated with the breeding
lineage.

Record, when available:

- family and cross;
- harvest information;
- quantity;
- notes;
- operational status.

Operational status is distinct from genetic purity and from archival state.

## Germination tests

Record:

- number of seeds tested;
- number germinated.

BreedOps calculates the germination rate from those counts.

## Good practice

Do not overwrite one physical lot to represent another harvest. Create distinct
lot records when the biological/material identity is distinct.
